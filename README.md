1. **Send your material once.** Slides, PDF, Word files or photos of handwritten notes.
2. **Approve a sample lesson.** I build one complete lesson first so you can check content, voice and visuals.
3. **Receive the finished course.** Videos, quizzes, handouts and landing page, ready to publish on your own Udemy account under your name.
